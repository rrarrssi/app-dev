## *YAMAHA YTX 125*

![image of YTX](https://motortrade.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/YTX125-BR21-BK.jpg)

>Yamaha YTX 125 is a budget-friendly, utility-focused motorcycle popular in the Philippines, priced around ₱49,900–₱57,900. It features a 125cc air-cooled engine, manual 4-speed transmission, and durable suspension designed for heavy loads, making it ideal for business and everyday transport. 

**General Overview**
- *Type:* Utility/Street motorcycle
- *Price Range:* ₱49,900 – ₱57,900 (Philippines market)
- *Colors Available:* Blue, Red, Black, White
- *Seating Capacity:* 2 riders
- *Purpose:* Affordable, reliable transport for personal and business use
