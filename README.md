## *YAMAHA YTX 125*

![image of YTX](https://motortrade.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/YTX125-BR21-BK.jpg)
[Source](https://motortrade.com.ph/motorcycles/yamaha-ytx-125/)

>Yamaha YTX 125 is a budget-friendly, utility-focused motorcycle popular in the Philippines, priced around ₱49,900–₱57,900. It features a 125cc air-cooled engine, manual 4-speed transmission, and durable suspension designed for heavy loads, making it ideal for business and everyday transport. 
---
**General Overview**
- *Type:* Utility/Street motorcycle
- *Price Range:* ₱49,900 – ₱57,900 (Philippines market)
- *Colors Available:* Blue, Red, Black, White
- *Seating Capacity:* 2 riders
- *Purpose:* Affordable, reliable transport for personal and business use

**Engine & Performance**
- Engine Type: Air-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 2-valve
- Displacement: 125 cc
- Cylinder Arrangement: Single cylinder
- Power Output: ~8 hp (6.0 kW) @ 7000 rpm
- Torque: 10 Nm @ 4500 rpm
- Compression Ratio: 10.0:1
- Fuel System: Carburetor
- Starting System: Electric starter & kickstarter
- Transmission: Constant mesh, 4-speed manual
- Clutch: Wet, multiple-disc

**Key Highlights**
- Affordable utility bike designed for carrying loads and business use.
- Durable suspension system supports heavy cargo.
- Simple, reliable mechanics make it easy to maintain.
- Fuel-efficient and practical for daily commuting.

# Conclusion
*The Yamaha YTX 125 stands out as a practical, budget-friendly utility motorcycle designed for riders who value reliability and simplicity over speed or luxury.
